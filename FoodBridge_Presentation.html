02 — Core Features

What FoodBridge Does

Six pillars of the platform — each solving a real-world challenge in food redistribution.

📊

Demand Forecasting

Moving-average algorithm predicts surplus per donor using historical data — shelters can plan meals 7 days ahead.

🗺️

Route Optimization

Batches multiple pickups into one efficient trip using nearest-neighbor algorithm — saves fuel and time for volunteers.

✅

Verified NGO Onboarding

Document upload, admin review, approval workflow, and community rating system ensures only trusted shelters participate.

🛡️

Food Safety Automation

Photo-based freshness checks at handover with color-coded badges (Fresh / Caution / Expired) and expiry countdown timers.

📈

Impact & CSR Reporting

Auto-generated monthly reports — meals saved, CO₂ avoided, per-donor breakdown. Downloadable for CSR and tax filing.

🌐

Multilingual & SMS Access

i18n support (English, Hindi, and more). SMS alerts for small shelters without smartphones via simulated Twilio integration.
